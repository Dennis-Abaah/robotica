OUR CURRICULUM

Where We Excel

Click any program track below to explore course details and hardware kits used.

01

mBot Robotics & Scratch Block Logic

Ages 6 – 9

Introduces younger kids to physical robotics using Makeblock mBot. Students learn how motors turn, ultrasonic sensors detect distance, and line-follower modules navigate custom maze maps.

  • Block Visual Coding
  • Ultrasonic Obstacle Avoidance
  • RGB LED Matrix Display
02

LEGO Spike Prime Mechanical Engineering

Ages 8 – 12

Combines colorful LEGO building elements, smart multi-port hubs, and high-torque motors. Students construct complex mechanical gears, robotic gripper arms, and automated sorting devices.

  • Gyro & Force Sensor Control
  • Gear Ratios & Linkages
  • Scratch to Python Transition
03

Arduino Microcontrollers & Embedded C++

Ages 12 – 17

Real electronics engineering for teens! Students learn circuit design on breadboards, resistor calculations, transistor switching, and text-based C++ firmware code directly on Arduino Uno & Nano microchips.

  • C++ Firmware Syntax
  • OLED & Sensor Interfacing
  • IoT & Wireless Controllers
04

AI and Prompting

Ages 10 – 17

Real-world AI use in developing solutions for problems around us.

  • Prompting
  • AI use cases
  • AI in developing solutions
